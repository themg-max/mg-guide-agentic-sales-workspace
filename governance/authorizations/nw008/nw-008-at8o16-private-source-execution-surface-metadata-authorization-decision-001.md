## 1. Decision scope

AT8O16 records a GRANTED human-governance authorization decision for the merged
AT8O15 request. This decision is strictly bounded to private execution-surface
metadata/schema inspection only and does not authorize execution of the
underlying private data operation.

Before merge of this decision unit, the authorization remains
`AUTHORIZATION_EFFECTIVE=NO` and `AUTHORIZATION_STATE=PENDING_MERGE`.
Merging is a prerequisite for activation and does not itself dispatch
inspection.

## 6. Separation from AT8O12

AT8O16 does not change or consume AT8O12:

```text

AT8O12_INSPECTION_ATTEMPTS_USED=0
ORIGINAL_AT8O12_INSPECTION_DISPATCHED=NO
```

AT8O16 authority is distinct from AT8O12 and does not grant the original AT8O12
inspection dispatch.
