## 2. Authority-origin finding

The open PR231 contract is not an acceptable authority origin. Its
registry-recognized owner-provisioning capability is minted by public
`note_path`, so a public caller can reach the issuer and the public module can
self-issue the authority it later verifies.

Deleting or unbinding that issuer before a genuine private origin exists would
leave no legal owner-provisioning path. Adding another public issuer would
recreate the same defect. A public-only repair therefore cannot close the
authority-origin gap.

```text
ROOT_FINDING=
  PUBLIC_OWNER_PROVISIONING_AUTHORITY_SELF_ISSUANCE

PRIVATE_MUTATION_REQUIRED=YES

PRIVATE_CONTROL_PLANE_ORIGINATES_PROVISIONING_AUTHORITY=YES
PUBLIC_RUNTIME_ORIGINATES_PROVISIONING_AUTHORITY=NO

PRIVATE_CONTROL_PLANE_ROLE=
  SOLE_OWNER_PROVISIONING_AUTHORITY_SOURCE
PUBLIC_NOTE_PATH_ROLE=
  VERIFIER_AND_CONSUMER_ONLY

PUBLIC_MODULE_CAN_ISSUE_OWNER_PROVISIONING_AUTHORITY=NO
PUBLIC_CALLER_CAN_SELF_DESIGNATE_OWNER=NO
PUBLIC_CALLER_CAN_MINT_CAPABILITY=NO
```

This authorization does not adopt, call, or depend on any public
owner-provisioning issuer. The future private unit must originate the capability
inside the existing designated private control plane. A later, separately
reviewed PR231 correction may remove the public mint and wire public
verification and consumption to that genuine private-origin capability.

