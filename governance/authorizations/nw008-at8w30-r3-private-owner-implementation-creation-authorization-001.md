## 2. Authoring-surface disclosure (placement)

The action that requested this artifact directed creation inside the private
source repository named by the approved private designation record. The
concrete private locator is private governance material and is held exclusively
by the human governance/private source custodian. It was not resolvable by the
authoring orchestrator, and no substitute source was selected.

```text

  SANITIZED_PUBLIC_GOVERNANCE_AUTHORIZATION_SURFACE

AUTHORIZATION_SURFACE_PRECEDENT=
  NW008_AT8W30_R3_PRIVATE_OWNER_EXECUTION_SURFACE_PROVISIONING_AUTHORIZATION_001

PRIVATE_MIRROR_REQUIRED_BEFORE_ACTIVATION=YES
PRIVATE_MIRROR_PERFORMED_BY=HUMAN_GOVERNANCE_SOURCE_CUSTODIAN
```

Rationale: this lineage authors sanitized authorizations for private-control-
plane work on the public governance surface, with the private locator never
disclosed and human governance performing the private-side merge. Choosing any
locally visible repository as "the" private source would have constituted owner
or designation reselection, which is forbidden by the bound designation.

Consequently this artifact is planning-only and cannot activate until the human
custodian mirrors it into the designated private governance surface and merges
it there.
