## 3. Blocking finding (P08–P11)

The merged public ingress requires the private-owner authenticity anchor to
satisfy a four-part contract before any private release. The merged private
control plane does not expose that contract, so the anchor can never be
accepted and the reserved consumer identities cannot be pre-bound by the
genuine designated private owner.

### 3.1 Public ingress anchor contract vs merged private surface

| Public ingress requirement | Merged private surface | Status |
|---|---|---|
| module-level `is_genuinely_provisioned(anchor)` callable | not defined | ABSENT |
| `anchor.provisioned_resolver` (must be a `ModuleType`) | not defined | ABSENT |
| `anchor.consumer_authorization_identity` (non-empty `str`) | not defined | ABSENT |
| `anchor.consumer_workflow_run_id` (non-empty `str`) | not defined | ABSENT |
