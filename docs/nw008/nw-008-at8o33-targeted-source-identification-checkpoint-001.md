## Purpose

This planning-only checkpoint defines a non-disclosing human-governance source
nomination record for AT8O33 evidence classes A, B, and C. It does not nominate
a candidate without supplied safe evidence and does not resolve source
authority from a missing nomination.

```text
A_SOURCE_AUTHORITY_RESULT=UNKNOWN
B_SOURCE_AUTHORITY_RESULT=UNKNOWN
C_SOURCE_AUTHORITY_RESULT=UNKNOWN
AUTHORIZED_GOVERNANCE_SOURCE_STEWARD_IDENTIFIED=UNKNOWN
```

A source nominator is not automatically the required binding steward. Any
binding-steward authority must have independent, preexisting, safe authority
provenance. A nominator's self-assertion cannot supply that proof.

## Controlled Vocabularies

```text
SOURCE_NOMINATION_RESULT_VOCABULARY=
SAFE_CANDIDATE_NOMINATED|
NO_QUALIFYING_CANDIDATE_KNOWN|
UNABLE_TO_DETERMINE

SOURCE_AUTHORITY_RESULT_VOCABULARY=
FOUND_EXISTING|
PROVEN_ABSENT|
UNKNOWN

NO_QUALIFYING_CANDIDATE_KNOWN_IS_PROVEN_ABSENT=NO
```

`SAFE_CANDIDATE_NOMINATED` requires every safe nomination field to be complete
and reviewable. `NO_QUALIFYING_CANDIDATE_KNOWN` records only the nominator's
bounded knowledge and does not prove absence. Without a qualifying nomination
or affirmative authoritative absence evidence, the authority result remains
`UNKNOWN`.
