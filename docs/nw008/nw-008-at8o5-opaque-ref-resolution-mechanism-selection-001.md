## 1. Purpose

AT8O5 compares only three opaque-ref resolution mechanism options and selects a
planning preference by applying the rule: choose the smallest reliable surface
that satisfies the complete authority retrieval contract.

## 2. Comparison framework

All options are evaluated against these mandatory requirements:

```text
PRIVATE_AUTHORITY_RETRIEVAL_EXACT_LOOKUP_REQUIRED=YES
PRIVATE_AUTHORITY_RETRIEVAL_EXACTLY_ONE_ACTIVE_RESULT_REQUIRED=YES

PRIVATE_AUTHORITY_RETRIEVAL_TRUST_VALIDATION_REQUIRED=YES
PRIVATE_AUTHORITY_RETRIEVAL_ADMISSIBILITY_VALIDATION_REQUIRED=YES
```

And these architecture/operations dimensions:
