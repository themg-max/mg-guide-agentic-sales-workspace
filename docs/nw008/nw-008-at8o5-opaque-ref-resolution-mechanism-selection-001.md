## 1. Purpose

AT8O5 compares only three opaque-ref resolution mechanism options. It identifies
a lead candidate by applying the rule to choose the smallest reliable surface
that satisfies the complete authority retrieval contract, but leaves the
preferred option unresolved until contract-fit evidence is available.
